Water Intake Tracker
live link :https://smart-water-tracker-mocha.vercel.app/
A simple water tracker that runs in the browser. Tap to add a glass, watch the glass fill up, and reach your daily goal.

Features
Tap "Add a glass" to log water, with an Undo button
Adjustable daily goal and glass size (150 to 500 ml)
Animated glass, falling droplets, and a celebration when you reach your goal
Streak button with current streak, best streak, and a 30-day view
Last 7 days chart
Light and dark theme
How to use
Open the live page 
Tap Add a glass each time you drink water.
Change your goal or glass size in the settings cards.
Tap the streak button to see your progress.
Tech
Plain HTML, CSS, and JavaScript in a single file (index.html)
No frameworks or installs needed
Data is saved in your browser (localStorage), so it stays on your device
